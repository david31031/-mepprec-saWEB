Who We Are

Our story starts
waay back....

Mepprec SA in itsself is an extension of the Main Franchise "MEPPREC" Located in DRC,Congo. We're an independent, Bible-believing church committed to raising people who know God for themselves, love their neighbour without condition, and carry hope into every space they walk into — Reveal Christ to the Nations and Impact a Maximum Amount of souls

01

Scripture First

The Bible is our final word on who God is and how we live — not opinion, culture, or convenience.

02

Radical Welcome

Wherever you're starting from, there's a seat for you before there's an expectation of you.

03

Family, Not Audience

Church is a body you belong to, not a service you attend. We're built for real relationship.

04

Nation-Minded

We exist for more than ourselves — raising leaders who carry faith into every sector of society.

First Time?

Everything You Need to Know Before Sunday

No insider knowledge required. Here's exactly what to expect, answered honestly.

Come as you are — jeans and a t-shirt are just as at home here as a Sunday best outfit. Nobody will look twice.

Mepprec Kids runs during both services for ages 0–12, in a safe, checked-in, dedicated space just off the main hall. Our team will show you there first.

Free parking is available on-site, with volunteers in bibs to guide you in on busy services. Overflow parking is signposted around Malanshof.
